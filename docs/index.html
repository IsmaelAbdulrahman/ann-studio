Get the ANN Studio app (Android)

The companion app is an Android app, currently in closed testing on Google Play — completely free. Here's how to get on the tester list:

1.  Use an Android phone or tablet.

2.  Email the author at ismael.abdulrahman@epu.edu.iq and ask to join the ANN Studio test. Send the Google (Gmail) account you use on your Android device — that address is what gets access.

3.  Once you've been added, open the app on Google Play and install it.

Open on Google Play

Because it's a closed test, only approved testers can install it — that's why the email step is needed. You'll get access shortly after the author adds you.

Free — with optional support
